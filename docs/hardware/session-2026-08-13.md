## 2. Record 21 30 — byte 18 FALSIFIED, byte 11 CONFIRMED, byte 1 promoted
Cold engine-off record: `61 30 00 2D 00 00 | 00 00 00 08 04 00 DD | 4D FF DD FF AE 00 00 |
06 18 00 08 00 00`
- **Byte 18 = STATUS BITFIELD, NOT temperature.** `0x06` engine-off → `0x86` the moment
  the engine fired (bit 7 = engine running; low bits constant 0x06). Yesterday's
  "84°C" was bit7+0x06 masquerading under raw−50. The X-Gauge byte-18/byte-0 lore is
  dead on this platform. (The falsified-decode gate had already kept it off the dash.)
- **Byte 11 = engine coolant echo at the TCU, CONFIRMED ×4** — tracked the engine's own
  0105 through the whole warm-up in lockstep: 27/—(off), 30/29, 50/50, 65/64, 90/89-90 °C.
- **Byte 1 = ONLY live trans-temp candidate.** Six samples over two days fit
  **ATF °C = 63 − raw** within ±1°: (raw→°C-est) 45→18 (cold soak = ambient ✓),
  40→23, 39→24, 33→30 (slow P-idle warm-up ✓ shape), 19→44, 18→45 (yesterday warm —
  plausible only if the drive was gentle; UNPROVEN). Prediction that decides it: ATF
  above 63°C forces raw to wrap SIGNED (e.g. 80°C → raw 0xEF=−17). 🖐 NEXT DRIVE TEST:
  one `2130` snapshot after a real drive — signed wrap appears exactly on schedule, or
  the model dies honestly.
- Byte pairs 12-13/14-15 (16-bit signed, moved −35/−82 off → +3 cold idle → −13..−1 warm,
  FFFC=−4 this session): converter-slip/current family per research; not temp. Uncataloged.

## 4. Car Scanner packet capture — the commercial playbook (full btsnoop)
Init: ATZ/ATE0/ATD0/ATH1/ATSP0/ATM0/ATS0/ATAT1/ATAL/ATST64. Then ~500 polls each:
- **"ATF temp" gauge = `21 30` @ 7E1 reading BYTE 11 — the coolant echo. FAKE ATF.**
  Displayed 192.2°F = coolant 89°C exactly (user caught it: "coolant is the same tho").
- **NO boost/MAP request of any kind** — no 010B, no mode-22 attempt. The commercial
  app has NO boost answer for this van either.
- **Oil temp = STANDARD PID `015C`** → `41 5C 81` = 89°C (coolant−1° at hot idle ✓).
  **OBD-35 SOLVED.** (Missed by our survey: 5C sits past the 0120 bitmap window.)

## 6. Process lessons
- Truncated ("filtered") HCI snoop is nearly useless for payloads — the Developer-options
  toggle set to full ("Enabled") + BT cycle is required; retrieve via `adb bugreport`
  (file may be NAMED btsnooz_hci.log while being standard btsnoop format).
- Our reconnect machine will out-compete third-party apps for the dongle — force-stop
  both our packages before handing the dongle to another app.
- Commercial-app packet capture (user's idea) beat both research agents and vendor
  databases for ground truth — one hour, three questions closed.
